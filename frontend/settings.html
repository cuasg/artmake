Basics
These affect every mode: ambient patterns, living drawing, pixel media, and camera mirror.
Recommended defaults
If you're not sure what to pick: Auto-tune FPS: On, Target FPS: 30, Max FPS cap: 60, Media FPS cap: 15, Camera FPS cap: 8–10.
Playback performance (FPS)
If you’re unsure: leave Auto-tune on. Lower FPS usually looks smoother on LED matrices than a jittery high FPS.
If playback feels “stuck” at a low FPS after experimenting, reset learned caps.
Living drawing (line art)
Controls for the animated line-art drawing mode.
Tip: “Generate / refine” tooling is being moved to Gallery where it belongs.
Pixel media & camera mirror
Pixel media renders photos/GIFs as full-color pixels. Camera mirror streams a live camera frame and pixelates it.
Crop behavior
Pixel media + camera mirror use “cover crop” to fill the matrix (e.g. 64×96 has no side bars).
